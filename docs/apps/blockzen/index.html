Block Zen: Grid Puzzle

Place blocks, clear lines, keep your calm. Classic, Daily, and Zen modes.

About this app

Block Zen is a calm, focused block-placement puzzle. Each turn you're offered three shapes; drag your attention to one, drop it onto the 10x10 board, and clear full rows or columns to keep the board breathing.
