Open source, research and evaluation

The company's research line sits where behavioural science meets AI systems: how people actually use them, where trust is earned and where it is faked, and how to measure the difference.

Research and evaluation

That research discipline is what the company sells as a service: quality, safety and robustness testing of models and agent systems, benchmark and protocol design, and expert review with the evidence recorded.

Separately, the director is a member of the expert group of Project 13 of the European Federation of Psychologists' Associations.

Clinical practice is separate from the company and is not offered through it.
