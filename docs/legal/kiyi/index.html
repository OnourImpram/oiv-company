KIYI: Night Drive privacy policy

Effective 31 October 2026. Google Play package: com.oiv.kiyi.

In short

KIYI: Night Drive is an arcade street racing game across coastal districts. You can play without an account. Your career progress, unlocked cars, upgrades and lap records stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; KIYI Night Pack (PRO) removes them. We do not sell data.

Who we are

KIYI: Night Drive is published by ONOUR IMPRAM VENTURES LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom under company number 17429906, which is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: onour@onourimpram.com.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app’s storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Game progress: career stage completion, stars, credits, unlocked vehicles, garage upgrades, contracts and lap records
  • Game settings and preferences: language, controls, camera, graphics quality and audio toggles

Service providers

  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version. The data it processes is described under Advertising below.
  • RevenueCat handles receipt validation when you buy KIYI Night Pack (PRO). It receives a random anonymous identifier generated for each install, your Google Play transaction receipt and your country, derived from the store you bought through. It does not receive your name or email address. Your game progress is never sent to RevenueCat.
  • Google Play processes the purchase itself under your Google account.
  • Crash and performance data come from Android vitals in Google Play Console.
  • Links you open are handed to your browser and that site's own privacy practices apply.

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

The free version of KIYI: Night Drive shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of every screen except the driving screen, an occasional full-screen interstitial ad after a race, and optional rewarded ads that you start yourself from the results screen for a +50% bonus on the credits you just earned. No ad appears while you are driving: the banner is hidden on the driving screen, and full-screen and rewarded ads start only from the results screen.

KIYI Night Pack (PRO) removes every ad. While KIYI Night Pack (PRO) is active, the app does not load, request or show any ad.

To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google: your device’s advertising ID; your IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location; device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version; diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs; and which ads you were shown and whether you tapped them. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention under its own privacy policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app first shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only when Google’s consent status allows it. If you do not agree to personalised ads, Google can still show non personalised ads, which use the same technical data for delivery, frequency limits, reporting and fraud prevention but are not based on your past activity. Outside these regions ads may be personalised. To change or withdraw your consent, email us; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. You can review or change your choice later from the privacy options in the app.

Your career progress, garage setup, scores and settings are never sent to AdMob or to any advertiser.

How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we do not do

  • Your game progress is not uploaded, and we run no server that stores your personal data.
  • No account is required.
  • The app does not collect precise location, your contacts, call or SMS history, health or fitness data, biometric data or microphone audio.
  • There is no usage analytics service in the app.
  • No data sale, and no sharing with advertising data brokers.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), used for ads and for the KIYI Night Pack (PRO) purchase.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS), declared by Google's Mobile Ads SDK and used by Google AdMob as described above. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Google Play billing (BILLING), used when you buy or restore KIYI Night Pack (PRO).
  • Vibration (VIBRATE), used for tactile driving and collision feedback.
  • Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK) and foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE), declared by the Google Mobile Ads and Play Billing libraries the app includes. Neither gives the app access to your personal data.

Your choices and deletion

Your records are stored on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them, unless you used your phone's own backup feature, which is outside our control. You can review or update your ad consent choices at any time from Settings (Privacy Options). Purchase records stay with Google Play and RevenueCat; to ask about those records, email us with the purchase's transaction ID. We respond within thirty days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Send requests by email; we will not ask for more information than we need to respond. In the United Kingdom you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

KIYI: Night Drive is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

We update this policy before any change to the app’s data practices is released. The effective date is shown at the top of this page.
