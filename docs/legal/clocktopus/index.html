Clocktopus privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026. Google Play package: com.brilliant.wellbeing.

In short

Clocktopus is a set of short lessons about everyday focus habits, told through a cartoon octopus. You can use it without an account. Your answers and progress stay on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Premium removes them.

Who we are

Clocktopus is published by ONOUR IMPRAM VENTURES LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom under company number 17429906, which is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: onour@onourimpram.com.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app’s storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your answers, points, the moves you choose, your check-ins and the notes you add to them, your Focus Capacity and the goal you chose. They are not uploaded, not backed up to us and not readable by us
  • Your language and reminder settings

Service providers

  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version. The data it processes is described under Advertising below.
  • RevenueCat works with Google to confirm what you have bought. It receives an anonymous identifier for your installation and the purchase history attached to it. It does not receive your answers or your goal.
  • Payment itself happens in Google Play. We never see your card details.
  • Links you open are handed to your browser and that site's own privacy practices apply.

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

The free version of Clocktopus shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the You tab, a banner on the page "How Focus Capacity is measured" and, now and then after you finish a lesson, a full-screen ad. No ad ever appears during a question.

Premium removes every ad.

To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google: your device’s advertising ID; your IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location; device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version; diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs; and which ads you were shown and whether you tapped them. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention under its own privacy policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app first shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only when Google’s consent status allows it. If you do not agree to personalised ads, Google can still show non personalised ads, which use the same technical data for delivery, frequency limits, reporting and fraud prevention but are not based on your past activity. Outside these regions ads may be personalised. To change or withdraw your consent, email us; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Nothing you answer or write in the app is ever shared with an advertiser.

How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we do not do

  • We use no analytics tool of our own in this app. We do not track which lessons you open or how you answer.
  • In the free version, the only data that leaves the phone for advertising is what the Advertising section describes.
  • There are no accounts, so there is nothing to sign in to.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), used for ads, Premium purchases.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS), declared by Google's Mobile Ads SDK and used by Google AdMob as described above. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Google Play billing (BILLING), used when you buy or restore Premium.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), only for the reminder, if you turn it on. It is scheduled locally by the phone itself, and you can turn notifications off for the app in Android settings.
  • Start at boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), declared by the notifications library so that scheduled reminders can be restored after the device restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE), declared by the haptics and notifications libraries the app includes.
  • Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK) and foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE), declared by libraries the app includes. Neither gives the app access to your personal data.
  • Launcher badge permissions (READ_APP_BADGE and the badge permissions of several device makers' launchers), declared by the notifications library for app icon badges. They give the app no access to your data.
  • Other manifest permissions (the Google install referrer service and the cloud messaging receive permission) are declared by libraries the app includes. The released build contains no Firebase project configuration.

Your choices and deletion

Your answers, points, moves, check-ins and settings are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Purchase records stay with Google Play and RevenueCat; to ask about those records, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Send requests by email; we will not ask for more information than we need to respond. In the United Kingdom you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

Clocktopus is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

We update this policy before any change to the app’s data practices is released. The effective date is shown at the top of this page.
