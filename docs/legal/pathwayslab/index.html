Pathways Lab privacy policy

Effective 6 October 2026. Google Play package: com.hezarfen.pathwayslab.

In short

Pathways Lab helps students test a possible career through a seven day experiment. You can use it without an account and the core experiment works offline. Your workspace stays on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; the optional Supporter Pack removes them. The app contains no analytics of its own and we do not sell data.

Who we are

Pathways Lab is published by ONOUR IMPRAM VENTURES LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom under company number 17429906, which is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: onour@onourimpram.com.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app’s storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Experiment progress, time and connection preferences
  • Curiosity notes, private check-in responses and conversation preparation
  • Saved opportunities and evidence you record
  • A temporary export file while you share it, removed during later cleanup or a full reset

Service providers

  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version. The data it processes is described under Advertising below.
  • RevenueCat is contacted when the app opens, to check whether you have the Supporter Pack, and when you choose to buy the optional Supporter Pack or restore a purchase. It may process an anonymous app user identifier, app and device metadata, product requests, purchase events and entitlement status. Career notes, check-ins, saved opportunities and evidence are never sent to RevenueCat.
  • Google Play processes the purchase itself under your Google account.
  • When you open an official opportunity page, the address is handed to your browser and that site's own privacy practices apply.

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

The free version of Pathways Lab shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the main screen and, at most once every three minutes, a full-screen ad when you complete a step of the day.

The Supporter Pack removes every ad. Each time Pathways Lab opens it checks your Supporter Pack with RevenueCat, and it shows no ads until that check has confirmed you do not have it.

To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google: your device’s advertising ID; your IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location; device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version; diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs; and which ads you were shown and whether you tapped them. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention under its own privacy policy.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app first shows Google’s consent form (User Messaging Platform) and requests ads only when Google’s consent status allows it. If you do not agree to personalised ads, Google can still show non personalised ads, which use the same technical data for delivery, frequency limits, reporting and fraud prevention but are not based on your past activity. Outside these regions ads may be personalised. To change or withdraw your consent, email us; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

Your career notes, check-ins, saved opportunities and evidence are never sent to AdMob or to any advertiser.

How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we do not do

  • The app does not ask for location, contacts, microphone, camera, notifications, health data or sensitive demographic information.
  • No analytics of our own and no data sale.

Permissions

  • Internet, used for ads, optional supporter purchases and pages you choose to open.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID), used by Google AdMob as described above. You can reset or delete it in Android settings.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), only for the optional daily reminder. It stays off until you turn it on in Settings, it is scheduled on your device and sends no data, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings or in Android settings.
  • Start at boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), only to keep your daily reminder scheduled after the device restarts.

Your choices and deletion

You can export your workspace and request a full local reset from Settings. The reset removes your workspace data and the temporary export from the device. It does not remove RevenueCat's own records or Google Play purchase records; to ask about those records, email us. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Send requests by email; we will not ask for more information than we need to respond. In the United Kingdom you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

Pathways Lab is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

We update this policy before any change to the app’s data practices is released. The effective date is shown at the top of this page.
