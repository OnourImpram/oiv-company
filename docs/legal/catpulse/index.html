CatPulse privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026. Effective 23 November 2026. Google Play package: com.hezarfen.catpulse. Samsung Galaxy Store package: com.hezarfen.catpulse.galaxy. iOS App Store bundle identifier: com.hezarfen.catpulse.

In short

CatPulse is a care companion for cat owners: daily care tasks, medication times, observations, veterinary visits and documents. You can use it without an account. Your cat profiles and care records are stored on your device. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; Premium removes them. We do not sell data.

Who we are

CatPulse is published by ONOUR IMPRAM VENTURES LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom under company number 17429906, which is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: onour@onourimpram.com.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app’s storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Cat profiles, daily care records, observations, tasks, medications and doses, veterinary visits and appointments, contacts and caregivers, inventory and expenses
  • Profile photos and documents you choose, copied into the app's own storage
  • Language, theme, onboarding state and reminder settings. The reminder scheduler also stores whether you had Premium when it last scheduled your reminders.

Service providers

  • Google AdMob shows ads in the free version. The data it processes is described under Advertising below.
  • RevenueCat checks whether you have Premium when the app opens. It may process product, transaction, receipt, subscription status and installation or store account identifiers, including an anonymous app user identifier, needed to grant, verify, manage and restore access when you buy or restore Premium through Google Play in the Google Play build, through Samsung In-App Purchase if you installed the app from Samsung Galaxy Store, or through the Apple App Store in the iOS build. The app gives RevenueCat no user ID of its own and does not send it your name, email address or other subscriber attributes, so RevenueCat works with its own anonymous identifier. Your cat profiles, care records, notes, photos and documents are never sent to RevenueCat.
  • Premium purchases are processed by the store you buy from and RevenueCat. Google Play processes the purchase itself under your Google account. If you installed the app from Samsung Galaxy Store, Samsung In-App Purchase processes the purchase under your Samsung account; we receive the purchase status, not your payment details. In the iOS build, the Apple App Store processes the purchase. Premium users can open the store's subscription management page from Manage subscription when a matching store link is available.
  • Sentry receives crash and error reports only when crash reporting is configured in the build. It also receives a small sample, about 5 percent, of performance measurements, including native frame rendering. Reports carry the error itself; reports of errors in the app's interface also include the component stack. The app turns off the Sentry SDK's default collection of personal data. For reports from the app's JavaScript code, it also drops console and network breadcrumbs, deletes request data and removes email addresses and phone-like numbers from messages before they leave the device. Sentry's documentation states that this filtering does not apply to native events from Android or iOS. Care records, notes, photos and documents are not attached on purpose. The same setup applies to the Android and iOS builds.
  • Links you open, such as article sources, are handed to your browser and that site's own privacy practices apply.

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

The free version of CatPulse shows banner ads from Google AdMob on the Home, Timeline and Learn screens, only while Google's consent status allows ad requests. The Home banner can appear before you add a cat. Timeline and Learn show a banner beneath the list when the list has items. On the Care insights screen you can also choose to watch one rewarded ad to preview the monthly view for that visit; nothing is loaded unless you tap that button.

Premium removes the banner ads and keeps the monthly view open without an ad. The banner request does not wait for the Premium check, so a banner can appear briefly when the app starts, and it appears if the check fails or does not answer.

To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google: a device or advertising identifier (on Android, your device's advertising ID; on iOS, the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking); your IP address, from which Google derives an approximate location; device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version; diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs; and your interactions with ads, such as which ads you were shown and whether you tapped them. Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention under its own privacy policy.

Every build runs Google's consent flow, the User Messaging Platform, when the app starts, before starting the Mobile Ads SDK. The app requests ads only when Google's consent status allows it. Ads are not requested while the check is running. If the check fails, the app uses the consent status already stored on your device and requests ads only if that status allows it. The app checks the status again when it returns to the foreground and before requesting a rewarded ad. If you do not agree to personalised ads, Google can still show non-personalised ads, which use the same technical data for delivery, frequency limits, reporting and fraud prevention but are not based on your past activity. When Google says privacy options are required, Ad privacy choices appears in Settings and reopens Google's consent form. A new answer takes effect immediately: ad requests start if they are now allowed and stop if permission is withdrawn. On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings; on iOS, you can allow or stop tracking in iOS Settings, as described below.

The app sends no cat profiles, care records, notes, photos or documents in ad requests. It does not add keywords or content URLs. The only ad request option it sets is the non-personalised ads option in the Galaxy Store build.

If you installed the app from Samsung Galaxy Store, it uses its own AdMob app and ad units, separate from the Google Play build. Ad requests are non-personalised in every region until explicit consent is recorded. The app records consent when Google's consent form reports that it has collected an answer. If Google's form has not collected one, no answer is stored, Google allows ad requests and you are not Premium, the app shows its own consent screen after onboarding. Yes and no are equally easy to choose, with nothing pre-selected. Leaving with the back gesture records nothing, so it returns at the next launch. The app keeps its own answer and a timestamp on your device only. You can change that answer with the "Personalized ads" switch in Settings while you are not Premium and Google's form has not collected an answer. If Google's form has not collected an answer and you say no, give no answer, or the answer cannot be read, requests stay non-personalised. A failed or slow check of Google's consent answer also keeps requests non-personalised. Your cat profiles, care notes, medications and documents are never sent to AdMob or to any advertiser.

On iOS, if the App Tracking Transparency question has not been answered yet, the app asks it immediately before it starts the Mobile Ads SDK, which it starts only when Google's consent status allows ad requests. This can happen when the app starts or after you give consent through Ad privacy choices. The app asks only while iOS has not recorded an answer; once you have answered, the app does not ask again. If you allow tracking, the Google Mobile Ads SDK can use your device's advertising identifier, IDFA, and share it with Google for the purposes described above. If you decline, Apple's documentation states that the advertising identifier returns all zeros instead of your device's identifier. Allowing or declining tracking does not decide whether ads appear: ads can still appear either way, subject to Google's consent status and your Premium status. CatPulse's own code does not read the IDFA, and its stated purpose for asking is to show ads that better match your interests. Cat profiles and care records are not used for advertising. You can change your answer later in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

What we do not do

  • Your cat profiles, care records, notes, photos and documents are not sent to us. They stay on your device unless you export a backup yourself.
  • The app does not ask for location, contacts, microphone or camera.
  • This version sends no usage analytics, and there is no data sale.

Permissions

The following list describes the Android builds. The iOS permissions are listed separately below.

  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE), used for ads, Premium purchases, crash reports and links you open.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS), declared by Google's Mobile Ads SDK and used by Google AdMob as described above. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Google Play billing (BILLING), used when you buy or restore Premium in the Google Play build.
  • Samsung In-App Purchase billing (com.samsung.android.iap.permission.BILLING), declared in the Galaxy Store build and used when you buy or restore Premium there. The Galaxy Store build also declares Google Play billing (com.android.vending.BILLING).
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), only for the care reminders you choose: task and medication times, and optional care nudges that follow your quiet hours. The app asks for this permission when you turn on reminders during onboarding, when you tap Enable device notifications or switch on Daily care digest, Weekly care recap or Personalized learning nudges in the notification settings, or when you set up a reminder yourself. After an explicit refusal, the app does not ask again on its own. Reminders are scheduled on your device and send no data, and you can turn each kind off in the app's notification settings or in Android settings. The app does not request a push notification token.
  • Start at boot (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED), only to restore your scheduled reminders after the device restarts.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE), used by reminder alerts.
  • Biometric or device credential (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT), only for the optional app lock you turn on. Android does the check and the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and older, and WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), declared by the photo and file libraries the app uses, for the photos and documents you choose and the backups you export or restore. Photos for cat profiles and the photo timeline are chosen through the system photo picker, and the app reads only the photo you choose.
  • Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK) and foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE), declared by libraries the app includes. They give the app no access to your personal data.
  • Launcher badge permissions (READ_APP_BADGE and the badge permissions of several device makers' launchers), declared by the notifications library for app icon badges. They give the app no access to your data.
  • Other manifest permissions (the Google install referrer service, the cloud messaging receive permission and DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION, an app-specific permission that keeps the app's own broadcast receivers private) are declared by libraries the app includes.

iOS permissions

  • Face ID, used only by the optional app lock. The device passcode can be used instead. The system checks your identity, and the app acts only on whether the check succeeded.
  • Photo library: you choose a cat profile photo or a photo for the timeline through the system photo picker. The app reads only the photo you select. It can use that photo's capture date to suggest a timeline date, but does not store the capture date separately.
  • Notifications, for local reminders scheduled on your device. The app asks for this permission when you turn on reminders during onboarding, when you tap Enable device notifications or switch on Daily care digest, Weekly care recap or Personalized learning nudges in the notification settings, or when you set up a reminder yourself. After an explicit refusal, it does not ask again on its own. The app does not request a push notification token.
  • App Tracking Transparency, for the advertising permission described above. The app asks only while iOS has not recorded an answer. Ads can still appear if you decline, subject to Google's consent status and your Premium status.

Your choices and deletion

You can export, restore or permanently delete your local records from Settings. Backups are not encrypted by CatPulse, so store and share them carefully. Deleting local records does not remove records held by RevenueCat, Google Play, Samsung In-App Purchase, Apple App Store, Sentry or Google AdMob; to ask about those records, email us. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data.

Data deletion

You can export, restore or permanently delete your local records from Settings. Deleting local records removes your care data, the files the app keeps for it and your scheduled reminders from your device; it does not remove records held by RevenueCat, Google Play, Samsung In-App Purchase, Apple App Store, Sentry or Google AdMob. Purchase records held by the store and RevenueCat are kept for as long as those processors' own policies require. To ask about those records, email us. Our address is onour@onourimpram.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Send requests by email; we will not ask for more information than we need to respond. In the United Kingdom you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

CatPulse is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

We update this policy before any change to the app’s data practices is released. The effective date is shown at the top of this page.
