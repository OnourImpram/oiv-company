Privacy Policy

Everything stays on your phone

Your book — every page, name and setting — is stored only on this device. Kinlore has no account, no server and no analytics, and never sends your writing anywhere.

Export and Share hand the text to the app you choose; from that moment it is that app's copy, under that app's rules.

The one-time purchase

Kinlore Premium is a single purchase through Google Play or the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by the store; Kinlore never sees your card.

To know whether the purchase belongs to this installation, Kinlore uses RevenueCat, which stores an anonymous identifier (shown as your Support ID in You) and the purchase record. No name, e-mail or other personal data is attached to it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on the Book tab and, now and then, a full-screen ad after a page is bound — never while you write. To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which Google derives an approximate location), device and app information, diagnostic data such as crash and performance logs, and which ads you see and tap, and shares them with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) asks you first and you can refuse personalised ads; non-personalised ads still use this technical data, but not a profile of you. Premium removes every ad. Nothing you write is ever shared with an advertiser.

What Kinlore does not do

No profiling of your writing, no location, no contacts, no microphone. Notifications, if you turn them on, are scheduled on the phone itself and carry only the next prompt.

Children

Kinlore is made for adults writing for the people they love and is not directed at children.
