Tiny Broadcast privacy policy

Effective 24 September 2026. Google Play package: com.hezarfen.tinybroadcast.

In short

Tiny Broadcast is a short session strategy game about running a small town radio station. You can play without an account. Your season is stored on your device. We do not sell data.

Who we are

Tiny Broadcast is published by ONOUR IMPRAM VENTURES LTD, a company registered in the United Kingdom under company number 17429906, which is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: onour@onourimpram.com.

Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only in the app’s storage on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your current season, completed days and results
  • Onboarding state and interface preferences
  • Local records of in game sponsor choices

Service providers

  • RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase state and purchase events when an in app purchase is offered, bought or restored through Google Play.
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform are included in the app. The current version does not request or show advertisements. As part of normal Android app startup, Google's SDK may process device and app information, such as the advertising identifier, under Google's privacy policy. If advertising is enabled in a future version, it will be limited to non personalised ads after a consent step where required, and this policy will be updated first.
  • Google Play processes purchases under your Google account.

RevenueCat privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • Program choices are game state. They are not used to infer sensitive traits or to build an advertising profile.
  • The game does not ask for contacts, location, microphone, camera, notifications or broad storage access.
  • In game analytics accepts only a bounded sponsor outcome value and is not sent to a remote analytics service in the current version.
  • No data sale.

Permissions

  • Advertising identifier access (AD_ID), declared by Google's Mobile Ads SDK. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier in Android settings.

Your choices and deletion

You can export and erase local game data in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes its local data. Purchase records stay with Google Play and RevenueCat; to ask about those records, email us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Send requests by email; we will not ask for more information than we need to respond. In the United Kingdom you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children

Tiny Broadcast is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Changes

We update this policy before any change to the app’s data practices is released. The effective date is shown at the top of this page.
