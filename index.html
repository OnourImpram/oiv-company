Onour Impram Ventures is a founder-run technology and research company registered in London. Research, evaluation and advisory

The company's research line sits where behavioural science meets AI systems: how people actually use them, where trust is earned and where it is faked, and how to measure the difference.

The director works in the European Federation of Psychologists' Associations' Project 13 expert group and has published two books in 2026, one on generative AI in mental health and one on positive psychology. That research discipline is what the company sells as a service.

Evaluation. Quality, safety and robustness testing of models and agent systems, benchmark and protocol design, expert review with the evidence recorded, for teams that need an answer they can defend.

Advisory and training. AI strategy and product judgement for organisations; research methods and evidence synthesis; workshops, courses and professional education, in English and Turkish.

Clinical practice is separate from the company and is not offered through it.
