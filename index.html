Onour Impram Ventures is a small software company registered in London. It designs, builds and publishes its own mobile applications — health tracking for pets, focus and routine tools, a prayer companion, a family memory book — and the research software behind them.

The company has one director and no outside investors. Everything it ships is released under its own name, with the same privacy rule in each product: data stays on the user's device unless the user asks otherwise.
