How the company works

Everything here is made by one person with a small machine room of tools. That sets the rule for what gets started: only work that can be finished properly, tested on real devices and kept alive after release.

Each application carries its own privacy notice and terms, written in nineteen languages, and the publisher of record is the company named below. Purchases run through the store's own billing, so the company never sees a card number.
