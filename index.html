Research and evaluation

The company's research line sits where behavioural science meets AI systems: how people actually use them, where trust is earned and where it is faked, and how to measure the difference. That research discipline is what the company sells as a service.

Evaluation. Quality, safety and robustness testing of models and agent systems, benchmark and protocol design, expert review with the evidence recorded, for teams that need an answer they can defend.

Separately, the director works in the European Federation of Psychologists' Associations' Project 13 expert group.

Clinical practice is separate from the company and is not offered through it.
